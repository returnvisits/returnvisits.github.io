Field Service Companion is a private notebook for the ministry. It helps you remember the people you meet, what you last talked about, and the conversation to continue next time.

The address is in your message, not on this page. Field Service Companion never sends names, addresses, or notes to our servers — what your friend shared travelled in the text message itself, and nothing about it was recorded here.
