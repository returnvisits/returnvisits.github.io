Something isn't working

If the app is behaving strangely, a debug report tells us what happened:

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Run integrity check now — this often clears things up on its own.
  3. If the problem continues, tap Share debug report and email it to us.

A debug report never contains your ministry records. It carries app events, error codes, and device information — never your contacts, visit notes, addresses, phone numbers, or anything you have written down.
